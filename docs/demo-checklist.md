1. Open WSC → Floor 1 → East → East hall tables (sample), then **Choose this spot**.
2. Select High noise and High crowding, enter a distinctive fictional note such as **Refresh proof 42**, and click **Confirm report**. Say: “This button sends the report to our Supabase database.”
